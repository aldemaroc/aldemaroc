# Aldemaro Campos

Electrical engineer. I spend my time on the layers most people never see: power, fiber, racks, and the routing that sits on top of them.

I run [Hostzone](https://hostzone.com.br), a Brazilian hosting provider I started in 2015. Own data center in Campina Grande, presence in Tier III facilities in São Paulo, out of **AS269070**.

Physical layer to BGP. I automate the rest.

## What I do

- **Network design and operation**: fiber, cross-connects, L2, BGP, IPv6, DDoS mitigation.
- **Electrical engineering**: power distribution, generation, UPS and high availability electrical systems.
- **Data center**: build and operate, from racks and cooling to the boring work that keeps uptime honest.
- **Automation**: provisioning, router policy, billing and monitoring wired together so nobody touches the same ticket twice.
- **Hardware and embedded**: build servers, break servers, play with microelectronics and firmware, write down what I learned.
